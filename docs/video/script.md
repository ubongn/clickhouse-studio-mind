fresh browser profile, zoom ~125–150%. No slides, no mockups — every frame is
the deployed app or the public repo.

- Repo: **https://github.com/ubongn/clickhouse-studio-mind**
- Narration: English, conversational, exec-tone. Lines below are the script —
  read naturally, don't robot them.

The one variable is shot 4's wait (45–90s warm, ~2min cold). If the live
answer takes 90s+, cut shot 9 (repo) to a 3s scroll-by and trim shot 6 to
one sentence. **Never cut shots 7, 8, or 11** — scan receipt, span tree, and
morning brief are the three things the judges cannot see anywhere else.

## Fallbacks

- Answer > 90s: the runbook's warm-up wasn't enough — retake after a second
  real `/ask`. If it persists, record locally (`python -m studio_mind.server`)
  and say "same code as the hosted deployment".
- Morning brief calm day (no watchlist): pin the incident day —
  `/morning?date=2026-05-21` — via the URL bar on camera: "let's look at the
  morning of May 21st". The planted CDN-incident week always lights up.
- Evidence card scrolled off: it renders under the brief; scroll slowly.

## Voice/tone rules

- Say "official mcp-clickhouse server" in full the first time (shot 4);
  afterwards "the MCP server".
- Numbers on screen do the arguing — narrate what to look AT, not the value
  itself when it's visible.
- Never say "demo data" or "fake"; the dataset is a **synthetic studio
  (Nimbus+, Nightfall Division)** — if asked, that's the phrasing.
- One breath of silence after shot 11's attribution line. Let it land.
